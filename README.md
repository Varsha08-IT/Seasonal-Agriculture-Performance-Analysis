# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing agricultural data to understand how agricultural performance varies across different seasons, crops, geographical regions, environmental conditions, farming practices, resource usage, and economic factors.

The analysis uses data analytics, statistical techniques, and data visualization to identify meaningful seasonal patterns, relationships, differences, and unusual observations in agricultural performance.

## Problem Statement

Agricultural activities are influenced by seasonal variations in environmental conditions, farming practices, resource availability, and market conditions. As a result, agricultural performance may differ from one season to another.

This project analyzes the given agricultural dataset to investigate seasonal differences in agricultural performance by identifying meaningful patterns, trends, relationships, and variations within the available data.

## Objectives

* Explore and understand the agricultural dataset.
* Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
* Examine agricultural performance across different seasons.
* Identify important seasonal patterns and trends.
* Investigate relationships between environmental conditions and agricultural outcomes.
* Compare agricultural performance across crops and regions.
* Analyze resource usage and irrigation patterns across seasons.
* Examine economic outcomes such as cost, revenue, and profit.
* Identify significant differences and unusual patterns.
* Apply appropriate statistical and visualization techniques.
* Develop evidence-based conclusions and recommendations.

## Dataset

The dataset contains agricultural information across different seasons, geographical areas, crops, and farming conditions.

It includes information related to:

* Farm and geographical details
* Crop and season
* Farm area
* Rainfall and temperature
* Humidity and sunlight
* Soil conditions
* Nutrient usage
* Irrigation methods
* Fertilizer and pesticide usage
* Seed quality
* Crop yield and production
* Market price
* Cost, revenue, and profit
* Water usage and water efficiency
* Disease and pest risk

## Data Preparation

The dataset was inspected for missing values and duplicate records.

Missing values were handled using median-based imputation for selected numerical variables. Duplicate records were also checked during the data-cleaning process.

After cleaning, the dataset was verified to ensure that the required variables were ready for analysis.

## Key Findings

* **Kharif** has the highest average yield among the three seasons.
* **Kharif** also has the highest average profit.
* **Zaid** has the lowest average yield and records a negative average profit.
* Crop performance varies considerably across seasons.
* **Sugarcane** has substantially higher average yield and profit compared with the other crops in the dataset.
